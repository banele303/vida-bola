Our Story & Heritage

A Family Farm Built With Purpose

Vidaboa Farm is an active family-run organic farm in Bronkhorstspruit, Gauteng. The Portuguese name "Vida Boa" means "Good Life" — reflecting our deep conviction that every South African family deserves access to real, wholesome, nutrient-dense food grown with care.

Across our 29 hectares, we nurture free-range broiler chickens, heritage runner hens, companion vegetable beds, fresh culinary herbs, and a growing goat herd. No synthetic sprays, no growth hormones, no shortcuts.

Our Journey

Growing Season by Season

Every harvest begins with patience. In 2020, our family acquired 29 hectares of Highveld land with a mission: to create a self-sustaining farm that provides clean nourishment and builds long-term community food security.

Through hard work, companion planting, and organic soil building, Vidaboa Farm has expanded from trial beds to supplying families, spaza shops, and markets across Gauteng and Mpumalanga.

2020

Acquisition of 29 Hectares

Acquired the Highveld property in Bronkhorstspruit. Initial land regeneration, water infrastructure, and soil mapping begun.

2021

Broilers & First Tomato Harvest

Trialling free-range broiler batches. Tomato crops thrived in animal-compost enriched soil, validating our organic methodology.

2022

Mixed Goat Herd Introduced

Introduced goats for rotational grazing and continuous organic compost production, further regenerating pasture quality.

2023 - 2024

Permanent Infrastructure & Expansion

Invested in predator-proof housing, irrigation, raised beds, and dedicated packing facilities.

2025 & Beyond

Direct Community Delivery

Direct delivery routes launched for Pretoria, Johannesburg, Bronkhorstspruit, and Witbank.
