# VidaBoa Farm 🌿

> **Highveld Organic Agriculture & Regenerative Family Farming**  
> Established 2020 · Bronkhorstspruit, Gauteng, South Africa

VidaBoa Farm is a 29-hectare organic agricultural farm dedicated to sustainable food sovereignty, chemical-free companion crop cultivation, pasture-raised free-range poultry, sweet tree-ripened Nana bananas, and community-first food supply across Gauteng and Mpumalanga.

---

## 🚀 Key Features & Pages

- **Home Page (`index.html`)**: Cinematic hero showcase, farm story, interactive WhatsApp produce order calculator, customer testimonials, and direct WhatsApp integrations.
- **Dedicated Products Catalog (`products.html`)**: Complete product listing with live vs dressed options, price indicators, quantity selectors, and one-click WhatsApp order generation.
- **Dedicated Nana Banana Store (`nana_banana.html`)**: Tree-ripened organic banana plantation showcase, household bundles, bulk harvest crates, and wholesale market boxes.
- **Farm Photo Gallery (`gallery.html`)**: 15+ real farm photographs with interactive category filtering (*Poultry & Livestock, Organic Crops, Nana Banana, Farm Life & Soil*).
